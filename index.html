I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy, which includes the limited data that the third-party services listed in Section 2 process to provide analytics, crash reporting and advertising.

1. Information Collection and Use

Birdays is designed with privacy in mind. Most features work entirely offline, and your personal data remains stored locally on your device.

A. Contacts Access (READ_CONTACTS)

The app requests permission to read your device's contacts (android.permission.READ_CONTACTS). This permission is used exclusively to enable you to import birthday dates and contact names from your address book into Birdays. All contact data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to my servers or any third parties.

B. Cloud Sync and Backup (Google Drive API)

If you explicitly choose to use the Google Drive backup and restore feature, the app utilizes the official Google Drive API to save and restore your backup file. The backup file is saved directly into your personal Google Drive account. I do not store, access, or have any visibility into your Google Drive files or account details.

2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for analytics, crash reporting, and advertising purposes. Each provider processes that data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services & Google Drive API
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Yandex Mobile Ads SDK
  • Yandex AppMetrica
  • Yandex Varioqub
  • Google Play Install Referrer

A. Advertising identifier and ad data

To load, show and measure ads, the advertising SDKs may access your device's advertising ID (the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission) together with basic technical data: device model, operating system version, language, coarse location derived from the IP address, and app interaction events such as ad impressions. This data is used to deliver and cap ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud; it is not used by me to identify you personally.

B. Turning ads off

Advertising can be disabled in the app settings (Settings → “Show ads”), and watching the rewarded video once also switches ads off. You can additionally reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android system settings.

3. Log Data and Crash Diagnostics

Whenever you use my Service, in case of an error or crash in the app, data and information are collected on your phone (through third-party tools like Firebase Crashlytics). This Log Data may include information such as your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device model, operating system version, app configuration at the time of the error, and other relevant diagnostic statistics.

4. Cookies

Cookies are files with a small amount of data commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. Birdays does not use cookies explicitly. However, third-party libraries (such as ad networks or analytics SDKs) may use cookies or similar tracking technologies to collect information and improve their services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Names, dates, phone numbers, e-mail addresses and notes you add are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are kept until you delete them. Deleting a record in the app removes it immediately, and uninstalling the app removes the local database and preferences. Backups created with Google Drive remain in your Google account and are deleted by you from Drive. Diagnostic and analytics data collected by the third-party services in Section 2 is retained according to their own policies; write to the address in Section 9 to request deletion of anything that can be linked to you.

6. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact me so that I will be able to take necessary actions.

7. Security

I value your trust in using my Service and strive to protect your data using commercially acceptable means. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.
