1. Information Collection and Use

Birdays is designed with privacy in mind. Most features work entirely offline, and your personal data remains stored locally on your device.

A. Contacts Access (READ_CONTACTS)

The app requests permission to read your device's contacts (android.permission.READ_CONTACTS). This permission is used exclusively to enable you to import birthday dates and contact names from your address book into Birdays. All contact data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to my servers or any third parties.

B. Cloud Sync and Backup (Google Drive API)

If you explicitly choose to use the Google Drive backup and restore feature, the app utilizes the official Google Drive API to save and restore your backup file. The backup file is saved directly into your personal Google Drive account. I do not store, access, or have any visibility into your Google Drive files or account details.

2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for analytics, crash reporting, and advertising purposes. Links to the privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services & Google Drive API

  • Yandex Mobile Ads SDK

3. Log Data and Crash Diagnostics

Whenever you use my Service, in case of an error or crash in the app, data and information are collected on your phone (through third-party tools like Firebase Crashlytics). This Log Data may include information such as your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device model, operating system version, app configuration at the time of the error, and other relevant diagnostic statistics.

4. Cookies

Cookies are files with a small amount of data commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. Birdays does not use cookies explicitly. However, third-party libraries (such as ad networks or analytics SDKs) may use cookies or similar tracking technologies to collect information and improve their services.
